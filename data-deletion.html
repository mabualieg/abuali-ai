Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of AbuAli AI, a personal AI assistant that communicates through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.

You may request deletion at any time, free of charge.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your data, send an email as described below:

  1. Email mabualieg@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: AbuAli AI Data Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, include the WhatsApp phone number you use with AbuAli AI, so we can identify the information to delete. Sending the request from an address you control, or referencing that number, helps us verify the request.
  4. Optionally, describe what you would like deleted (for example, all data, or specific messages or files).
You can also send the word DELETE as a message directly to AbuAli AI on WhatsApp to signal a deletion request. We recommend following up by email so we can confirm completion.

What Gets Deleted

Upon a verified request, we will take reasonable steps to delete personal information associated with your use of the Service, which may include:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number as stored by the Service.
  • The content of messages you sent to and received from the assistant.
  • Files and voice notes you submitted, and any text derived from them.
  • Related processing logs stored by the automation workflow, where reasonably identifiable to you.

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or maintain the security and integrity of the Service. Information already shared with third-party providers (such as the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform or AI providers) is handled according to those providers' own retention and deletion practices; you may need to contact them directly regarding data they hold.

Response Time

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving them and to complete deletion of the associated information promptly thereafter, subject to the retention exceptions described above.

Contact

For any questions about data deletion or this process, contact mabualieg@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
