AbuAli AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. About AbuAli AI

AbuAli AI ("AbuAli AI", "the Service", "we", "us") is a personal AI assistant. It communicates with its user primarily through WhatsApp, using the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform. Automation and message handling are performed through a self-hosted n8n instance.

AbuAli AI is currently operated mainly for private, personal use. This policy describes how information may be processed when you interact with the Service, and is written to remain applicable if access is extended to additional users in the future.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on how the Service is used, the following categories of information may be processed:

WhatsApp messages and phone numbers

  • The WhatsApp phone number associated with your account, as provided to us through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • The content of messages you send to and receive from AbuAli AI, including text messages, commands, and instructions.
  • Basic metadata associated with messages, such as timestamps and message identifiers.

Files and voice messages voluntarily submitted by users

  • Files, documents, and images you choose to send to the assistant.
  • Voice notes or audio messages you choose to send, and any text generated from them (for example, transcriptions) so the assistant can act on them.
  • Any other information you intentionally provide in the course of using the assistant.

You should only send information that you are comfortable having processed by the Service and the third-party providers described below. Please avoid sending highly sensitive information unless it is necessary for the task you are requesting.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed in order to:

  • Receive, understand, and respond to your messages, commands, and requests.
  • Perform the tasks and automations you ask the assistant to carry out.
  • Process files and voice messages you submit so the assistant can act on their contents.
  • Maintain, operate, debug, and improve the reliability of the Service.
  • Protect the Service against misuse, abuse, and security issues.

4. AI Processing and Automation

AbuAli AI relies on automated processing to interpret your messages and generate responses. Message content and related information may be passed through a self-hosted n8n workflow and, where applicable, to AI providers in order to produce a response or complete a requested task.

Because responses are generated automatically, they may not always be accurate or complete, and they should not be relied upon as professional, legal, financial, or medical advice.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate, AbuAli AI depends on third-party providers. When information is shared with these providers, it is limited to what is needed to deliver the requested functionality, and each provider's own terms and privacy practices apply to the data they handle.

Meta WhatsApp Business Platform

Messages are sent and received through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes message delivery and associated metadata in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's policies.

AI providers (OpenAI and/or Anthropic, where applicable)

To generate responses, message content and relevant context may be sent to AI providers such as OpenAI and/or Anthropic. These providers process the submitted content to return a response and handle it under their own terms and privacy policies. AI providers may be added, changed, or removed over time as the Service evolves.

n8n and infrastructure providers

Automation is handled through a self-hosted n8n instance running on server infrastructure. Hosting, storage, and networking providers that support this infrastructure may process information as part of operating the Service. Where the assistant is configured to do so, it may also connect to additional services you authorize, such as email, calendar, databases, files, or other APIs, in order to complete tasks you request.

6. Data Storage and Logs

To operate reliably, the Service may store message content, submitted files, voice notes, and processing logs for as long as reasonably necessary to provide functionality, maintain conversational context, debug problems, and keep the Service secure. Logs may be generated by the automation workflow and supporting infrastructure.

We aim to retain information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, and to remove or reduce data that is no longer required. We do not commit to a fixed retention period, as needs may vary by feature and over time.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With the third-party service providers described above, to the extent needed to operate the Service and complete your requests.
  • With services you explicitly authorize the assistant to connect to on your behalf.
  • Where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • Where necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users, the operator, or the public.

8. No Sale of Personal Information

AbuAli AI does not sell your personal information. Information is used only to operate the Service and provide the functionality you request, as described in this policy.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information processed by the Service against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use the Service at your own discretion and are responsible for the information you choose to share with it.

10. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access information associated with your use of the Service.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Request deletion of information associated with you (see Section 11).
  • Withdraw from using the Service at any time by ceasing to message the assistant.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in Section 14.

11. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of AbuAli AI at any time. Detailed steps are provided on the Data Deletion Instructions page. In summary, you can send a deletion request to mabualieg@gmail.com from, or referencing, the WhatsApp number you use with the Service, and we will take reasonable steps to delete the associated information, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate operational reasons.

12. Children's Privacy

AbuAli AI is not directed to children and is not intended for use by individuals who are under the age required to consent to the processing of personal information in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to the Service, please contact us so it can be removed.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Service, its providers, or applicable requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
